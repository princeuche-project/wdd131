What is Nutrition?

Nutrition is the process by which the body obtains and uses nutrients from food to support growth, energy production, tissue repair, and overall health. A nutritious diet provides essential nutrients such as carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, and water, all of which play important roles in maintaining bodily functions. Good nutrition helps strengthen the immune system, supports healthy development and improves physical and mental performance also reduces the risk of many diseases. Eating a balanced variety of foods, including fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats. The key to achieving proper nutrition and maintaining a healthy lifestyle.
